No holding your money

Common questions

Do I need my own Stripe account?

Yes — payments go directly to your Stripe account, so you get paid instantly without any holding periods. If you don't already have one, it takes about five minutes to set up at stripe.com.

How long does it take to go live?

For most venues, under 24 hours. We handle the initial setup — you give us your event details, ticket types, and pricing and we take it from there.

Does the POS require special hardware?

The POS interface runs on any tablet or laptop. For card payments you'll need a Stripe Terminal WisePOS E reader ($249). Cash payments work without any additional hardware.

Can I use just one product, or do I need all three?

Use as much or as little as you need. Some venues just need event ticketing. Others add registration forms for leagues and tournaments. The POS is an add-on for venues that also want to manage bar or merch sales.

Is C8 Tickets cheaper than Eventbrite?

For most small venues, yes. Eventbrite charges a percentage of every ticket plus a per-ticket fee, and holds your payout until after the event. C8 Tickets has no monthly fee and payments go directly to your Stripe account as tickets sell. Contact us and we'll run the numbers for your specific event size.

What does it cost?

We tailor pricing to your venue's size and which products you need. Reach out and we'll put together a proposal based on your specific situation.

Is there a contract or minimum commitment?

No long-term contracts. We believe if the platform is working for you, you'll stick around.
